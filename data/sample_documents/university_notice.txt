The University has announced that semester examinations for all undergraduate
programs will begin on October 15. Students are advised to download their
examination hall tickets from the student portal starting October 8. The last
date for registration is October 5, and no late registrations will be accepted
under any circumstances. Students with pending fee dues must clear them before
October 4 to be eligible for the examinations.

The examination schedule has been published on the department notice board and
the university website. Examinations will be held in the morning session from
9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and in the afternoon session from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
Students must carry their university ID cards and hall tickets to the
examination hall. Electronic devices, including mobile phones and smart
watches, are strictly prohibited inside the examination premises.

The Controller of Examinations stated that results are expected to be declared
within six weeks of the final examination. Re-evaluation requests can be
submitted online within ten days of the declaration of results, with a fee of
500 rupees per subject.
